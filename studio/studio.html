Hands

Drag from a socket to a card to lay a cord, or tap a socket and then the card. Tap a cord to select it, then × or Delete to remove it. The lettered pips beside each output wire it to an Olvasó, or unwire it.

Make a processor unit

A processor is clockwork between an outside source and Olvasó: it turns what comes in into a few numbers and never learns or names anything. Start from a use case, then adjust.
