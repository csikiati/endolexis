Make a guard unit

        
A guard stands between Olvasó's wanting and the world, and Olvasó never learns that it exists. Its rules can only take away: refuse, limit, stop a hand at work, or ask a person first. It reads its own signals, never Olvasó's words. A signal the body cannot sense counts as danger unless you say otherwise. Write a scenario test for every rule.
